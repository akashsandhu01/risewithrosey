About

Rosey Sharma, MEd, CCC

Counsellor · Canadian Certified Counsellor · 18 years in practice

Welcome to Rise with Rosey Counselling. With nearly 20 years of experience, I bring a deep commitment to supporting children, youth, adults, and families through meaningful and compassionate therapeutic work.

I am a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC) and have been a certified member of the Canadian Counselling and Psychotherapy Association since 2008. I am deeply committed to culturally inclusive counselling and strive to create a safe, respectful space where clients feel seen, heard and understood. My approach is grounded in empathy, curiosity, and respect, with the belief that meaningful change happens through connection.

I work alongside clients on their journey to heal, grow, and deepen their understanding of themselves and their world. I support women navigating life transitions, parents seeking connection through an attachment-informed lens, individuals struggling with mood disorders, experiencing grief, and the experience of how trauma can show up across the lifespan.

Whether you're seeking support during a challenging season or wishing to build resilience and self-awareness, therapy is tailored to meet your unique needs and goals. My work integrates both evidence-based strategies and a holistic perspective — balancing "heart and head" — to support emotional insight with practical skills, and overall well-being.
