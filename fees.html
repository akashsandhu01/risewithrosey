FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are sessions online or in person?

Therapy sessions are offered online across British Columbia. In-person sessions are available only for clients doing Sandtray therapy, in Delta, BC.

What ages do you work with?

Rosey works with children (6 to 10), preteens, teens and adults — as individuals, couples and families.

Will my insurance cover sessions?

Many extended health plans in BC cover counselling by a Canadian Certified Counsellor (CCC). Coverage varies by plan, so check with your provider — and feel free to ask Rosey to help verify your coverage before your first session.

What happens in the free consultation?

It's a relaxed, no-pressure 15-minute phone or video conversation. You can share a little about what brings you to therapy, ask questions about Rosey's approach, and decide together whether it feels like a good fit.

Do you offer sessions in Punjabi?

Yes — sessions are available in both English and Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ).

What is Sandtray therapy?

Sandtray therapy uses a tray of sand and miniature figures to externalize inner experiences — it's especially powerful for children, youth and anyone who finds it easier to show rather than tell. Rosey is a Level 1 NSST Practitioner (Neuroscience and Satir in the Sand Tray).

What is EMDR?

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is an evidence-based therapy that helps the brain process and integrate traumatic memories, reducing their emotional charge over time.

How do I pay?

Payment is by e-Transfer, due at the time of your session. A receipt is provided that you can submit to your extended health plan if you have coverage.

Have a question that isn't answered here?

Reach out — Rosey is happy to answer any questions during your free consultation.
