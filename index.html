Who I work with

Support for every season of life

Whether you're moving through a challenging chapter or building resilience and self-awareness, therapy is tailored to your unique needs and goals.

Children, youth & families

Specialized child, youth and family therapy — including play therapy, Sandtray and attachment-informed parenting support that builds connection at home.

Women in transition

Support for women navigating life transitions — pregnancy and postpartum, grief, relationships, identity, and the changes that reshape who we are.

Trauma across the lifespan

EMDR and trauma-focused therapy for processing painful experiences — with care for how trauma can show up at every stage of life.

My approach

Heart and head, together

"I strive to create a safe, respectful space where clients feel seen, heard and understood."

My work integrates evidence-based strategies with a holistic perspective — supporting emotional insight alongside practical skills and overall well-being. Grounded in empathy, curiosity, and respect, and deeply committed to culturally inclusive counselling.
