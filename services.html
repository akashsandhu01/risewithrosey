Services & specialties

Therapy tailored to your needs and goals

Individual, couples and family sessions — online across British Columbia, with in-person Sandtray therapy in Delta.

How we can work together

Session formats

Individual therapy

One-on-one support for children (6+), preteens, teens and adults — for anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, life transitions, self-esteem and more. Offered online.

Couples therapy

Relationship, marital and premarital counselling drawing on the Gottman Method and attachment-based approaches to rebuild connection and communication.

Family therapy

Support for family conflict, parenting challenges and behavioural issues — helping families connect through an attachment-informed lens.

Sandtray therapy (in person): As a Level 1 NSST Practitioner (Neuroscience and Satir in the Sand Tray), Rosey offers in-person Sandtray sessions in Delta, BC. All other sessions are held online.

Treatment approach

Evidence-based, holistic, and human

Therapy is never one-size-fits-all. Rosey draws from a broad toolkit and shapes each session around what works for you.

EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing — an evidence-based approach that helps the brain process traumatic experiences.

Sandtray & play therapy

Neuroscience and Satir in the Sand Tray (NSST), Sandplay and play therapy — powerful, hands-on approaches especially for children and youth.

Attachment-based

Understanding how early bonds shape our relationships — and helping parents and partners build secure, lasting connection.

CBT & DBT

Cognitive Behavioural and Dialectical Behaviour therapies for reshaping unhelpful thought patterns and building emotion-regulation skills.

Couples & family methods

The Gottman Method, family/marital therapy and narrative approaches for stronger relationships and family systems.

Strength-based & solution-focused

Person-centered, motivational interviewing, solution-focused brief therapy (SFBT) and trauma-focused work — always building on your strengths.
